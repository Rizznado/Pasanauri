Why “Pasanauri”?

Named after a village
on the Military Road

Pasanauri sits in the Aragvi valley on the road north to Kazbegi, and it’s where Georgians say khinkali were born. We keep that mountain recipe — thin dough, juicy filling, pleated by hand — and serve it a few minutes from Rustaveli.

A small ritual

How to eat khinkali

01 / 04

  1. 01

    Hold it by the knot

    Hands only — forks and knives would let the broth escape. Flip it upside down by the twisted top (the “kudi”).

  2. 02

    Take a small first bite

    Nibble a little hole in the side. Careful — it’s hot inside.

  3. 03

    Sip the broth

    This is the best part. Not a drop on the plate — that’s the rule.

  4. 04

    Leave the knot

    Finish the rest, put the top on your plate and count your knots. Black pepper on top is a must.
